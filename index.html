What we are building now.

FLAGSHIP GAME PROJECT

ProjectMercenary

ProjectMercenary is a systems-driven tactical auto-battler in development. Players recruit mercenaries, shape formations, manage town facilities, and progress through competitive and PvE content built around readable combat and long-term progression.

  • Built with Unity and a modular, systems-first architecture.
  • Focuses on squad composition, positioning, progression, and combat readability.
  • Includes town management, league battles, expedition content, and mercenary development.

PLATFORM CONCEPT / PRODUCT LINE

PrintForge Marketplace

A platform concept that brings together people who own 3D printers and people who want products manufactured. The system is designed to support marketplace operations, production-cost calculation, commission visibility, and AI-assisted analysis for listings, product quality, and sales recommendations.

  • Connects makers, printers, and buyers inside a single production marketplace.
  • Calculates production costs, marketplace commissions, and operational margins.
  • Uses AI to review listings and suggest improvements for pricing, positioning, and presentation.
